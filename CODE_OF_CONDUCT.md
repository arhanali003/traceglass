# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and patient. Critique the change, not the contributor.
Help newcomers reproduce problems and understand review feedback. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, doxxing and publication of private code or credentials
are not acceptable.

The repository owner moderates project spaces and may remove harmful content,
restrict participation or block repeated abuse. Decisions should be proportionate
and explainable. Report inappropriate public content with GitHub's reporting tools;
for sensitive concerns request a private contact channel without posting personal
details. No independent moderation team or confidential reporting service is
currently established.
