# Investigation report

Scale the report to the question. A useful compact format:

## Answer
The result in plain language, with its evidence level.

## Target and scope
Artifact/version/digest or source revision; requested question; inspected boundary; method and tool version when relevant.

## Findings
| Claim | Evidence | Status | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific result | Path:line, address, Evidence ID, or observation | Observed / Inferred / Unknown | Coverage or conditions |

## Trace
Entry point → transformation → output, with references at important steps.

## Open questions
Unresolved question and the smallest next check. For broad requests, account for every requested question.

When implementing from a report, add observed requirements, implementation decisions, and verification separately. Never fill an evidence field with a fabricated tool result.
